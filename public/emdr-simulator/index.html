Before you start

This simulator renders light programmes on your screen. A few built-in programmes are deliberately unsafe so you can test the firmware limiter.

A display guard runs at all times. If the output would flash faster than 3 times per second, the 3D view shows a time-averaged image instead. It does this even when you switch the device limiter off.

Material coefficients are nominal starting values. Calibrate them against your own resin and diffuser samples before you rely on absolute numbers.
